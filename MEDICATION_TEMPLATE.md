# ICU Medication Canonical Template & Quality Standard

Every medication in the ICU Knowledge Map must fulfill this 14-section structure at full medical depth.

## 1. Metadata
- **ID & Slug**: Unique URL-friendly string (e.g. `ertapenem`, `succinylcholine`, `tmp-smx`)
- **Name & Generic Name**: Standard international name
- **Brand Names**: Common reference brand names
- **Class & Category**: Subcategory classification
- **Summary**: Concise one-liner high-yield bedside summary

## 2. Mechanism & Target Diagram
- **Drug → Target → Subcellular Effect → Bactericidal / Physiologic Effect**
- Visual diagram label for teaching components

## 3. Antimicrobial Spectrum (Anti-Infectives)
- Gram-Positive, Gram-Negative, Anaerobic, Atypical/Fungal coverage
- **NOTABLE GAPS**: Explicit callouts of non-covered organisms (e.g., Pseudomonas for Ertapenem)

## 4. Pharmacokinetics & Pharmacodynamics (PK/PD)
- PD Type: Time-dependent ($fT > \text{MIC}$), Concentration-dependent ($\text{Peak}/\text{MIC}$), AUC/MIC, or Receptor occupancy
- Half-life, Protein binding, Volume of distribution ($V_d$), Metabolism, Clearance route

## 5. ICU Clinical Reasoning
- **Why I Choose It** (specific clinical indications & advantages)
- **Why I Don't** (gaps, toxicities, contraindications)
- **Bedside Pearl** (high-yield clinical takeaway)

## 6. Dosing & Administration
- Routine ICU regimen
- Adult Intubation / Emergency Airway Dosing (for NMBAs/Sedatives)
- Syndrome-specific dosing (e.g., Bacterial Meningitis 2.0 g IV q12h for Ceftriaxone; PJP 15–20 mg TMP/kg/day for TMP-SMX)
- Renally adjusted dosing table ($CrCl$ ranges)
- Hemodialysis & CRRT dosing protocols

## 7. Organ Failure Considerations
- Renal Failure & AKI considerations
- Hepatic Impairment considerations

## 8. Toxicity & Safety Warnings
- Major warnings & Boxed Warnings
- Organ-system toxicities (Nephrotoxicity, Ototoxicity, Neurotoxicity, Rhabdomyolysis, etc.)

## 9. Bedside Drug Interactions
- Drug → Mechanism → Consequence → Mandatory ICU Action

## 10. Monitoring & TDM Protocols
- Clinical & laboratory monitoring parameters
- Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM) goals (Peak, Trough, AUC targets) where applicable

## 11. Antimicrobial Stewardship & De-escalation
- De-escalation triggers & narrowing guidance
- "Broader ≠ Better" teaching point

## 12. Evidence & Claim-Level Citations
- Linked guidelines (IDSA, ATS, ASHP, ESCMID) & major trials
- Direct claim-level citations with source references

## 13. Patient Scenarios
- High-yield bedside clinical scenarios demonstrating decision making in complex physiology

## 14. Exam Traps & Bedside Misconceptions
- Common clinical traps, board misconceptions, and evidence-based reality checks
